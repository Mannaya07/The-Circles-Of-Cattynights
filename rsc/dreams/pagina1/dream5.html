Once you passed the bridge I talked about, on the right there was a place where you couldn't go any further. It was like a place shaped like an "L". In this place, there was a door, and there was a yellow sign saying that it was a cinema. I remember entering this place. To complete this parkour, I had to do wall jumps through purple glowing black holes. The place was completely dark, but I was able to see. I remember failing this parkour a lot of times, and because of that, the entity teleported me to the other side. He gave me a quest to do. I remember the quest appearing in front of my eyes, like a UI, printed in my pupil. I don't remember what that quest said, but I remember exiting that place, leaving the cinema and doing the quest. I remember that he was there, but I don't remember anything else. This time, I entered through the right door, the one for normal people, and I don't remember much about the cinema, but I remember that it was like a normal cinema, not a big one.
