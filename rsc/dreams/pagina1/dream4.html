I was hanging out with my friends. We were at the seaside, chilling, talking, and having fun. At one point, we looked up at the sky and watched it turn to night. "It had been daytime, and it was a sunny day." The sky turned into an incredibly clear night sky, and we were able to see every single star, everything in the sky. It was beautiful. At one point, I noticed cracks starting to appear in the sky, as if it were slowly breaking apart. Light, slightly brighter than the darkness of the black night sky, was coming out of these cracks. A few seconds after the cracks started appearing, I heard something like an explosion, and we began levitating towards the sky. We got very close to it, and at that moment, everything went completely dark. I woke up again in what looked like a UFO. I was alone, but through a microphone, I was still able to communicate with my friends. I was trying to understand what was happening. "I remember being inside the UFO with a girl, but I don't really remember who she was. I can barely even remember whether I was alone or with a girl." Anyway, I was panicking really badly, and I kept thinking to myself that this would probably be the last day of my life. Then, I noticed that the UFO had windows through which I could see where I was. I looked out of the window and saw that I was falling towards a planet at a speed that seemed close to the speed of light. At that moment, the feeling that this was the last day of my life grew stronger and stronger. Eventually, I hit the ground of that planet. I didn't die, but the UFO was destroyed. The UFO's door was open, and I heard some distant voices approaching me. They were male voices. They came closer to the UFO and looked inside to see what it was. They were soldiers. I immediately got scared because they were armed, and I didn't even know where I was. However, they looked at me strangely and then walked away. They were completely human, just like us. After that, I got out of the UFO and realized that I was in a world that looked like it was from around the year 1290 or something like that. There was no technology, and almost everywhere I looked, I could see farmers working, shepherds, and old churches. I remember seeing a woman planting tomatoes. She looked at me and introduced herself, so I did the same. I remember her speaking my language. After we introduced ourselves, I asked her if she wanted some help, so I helped her. While I was helping her, she asked me what I had on my wrist. I looked at my wrist and realized that I still had my smartwatch. That's when I understood that I wasn't in my own time, but in a much earlier period. After realizing that, everything went black again. Maybe I was inside a spaceship again. Everything went black once more, and I woke up in a house. There was no way to escape from this house, and I was living with this strange family whose members looked like humanoid kangaroos. They didn't speak, but they didn't seem hostile either. The house looked like my old house, the one I used to live in. I remember trying to leave the house, but I wasn't successful. They started feeding me, so I accepted the food. I remember that I still had my smartwatch, "the same one I have in real life," and that the fridge in that house was a digital fridge. I don't remember anything else after that.
