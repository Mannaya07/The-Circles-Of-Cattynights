ABOUT THE MUSIC PLAYER



The main site, open page's in a new tab, so you can mantain this one open, with the musics you want to listen while you go around the site.

you can pick a song from your device and let it loop, or choose a whole folder to shuffle through your music. every song is played once before the playlist reshuffles and starts over. songs are picked randomly, but no song will repeat until all of them have played. then the whole process starts again with a new random song!

you can pause, skip songs, adjust the volume, and seek through tracks whenever you want.

your music stays on your device and nothing gets uploaded. the player stops when you leave the page, so make sure to keep the main page open while you explore the rest of the site!
